About

What Tecirc explores, and where the name comes from.

Name

Tecirc combines the words Tech and Circle.

Starting with a technology or product, I look at the design around it, the experience of the people using it, and the intentions of its creators. The name means a place to gather around technology and think about it.

Concept

On Tecirc, I write about what I notice and wonder about while using gadgets, services, and AI.

Alongside specifications and new features, I look at interfaces, design, usability, and the reasons behind a feature.

This site goes beyond reviews and news. It is a record of my thinking, based on personal experience and research.
